Hi. I'm NinjaVoin.

Real name Nikita Rozumnyi. I make things, mostly on screens, sometimes in boxes, occasionally on the street.

Six years of making things on the internet, from somewhere between New York and New Jersey.

Voin.Studio is the name on the door, but it's just me behind it. I design, I build, I film, I draw letters. I also get bored easily, which turns out to be a useful trait: if something could be more fun, I usually try.

House rules.

Boring is the only real mistake.

Built from scratch, never from a template.

The details nobody notices are the ones everybody feels.

A menu, a website, a pothole. Same obsession.
